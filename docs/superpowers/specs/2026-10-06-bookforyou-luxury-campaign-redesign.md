# BOOKFORYOU — Luxury Campaign Redesign

## Goal

Replace the restrained editorial MVP presentation with a premium, conversion-oriented campaign site that makes the release feel desirable and collectible. Preserve the original TЗ restrictions: cultural, non-mystical, no vintage-book clichés, no gold frames, no fake commerce, and mobile priority.

## Direction

**Luxury Campaign:** fashion-house / niche-object launch language, expressed through oversized typography, strict editorial grid, deep wine contrast, a prominent release/price plaque, and fast purposeful motion. The physical book kit is supporting texture, not the hero's main attraction.

## Header

- Prominent header integrated into the campaign, not a thin utility bar.
- Desktop: BOOKFORYOU wordmark, `COLLECTION / STORY / GIFT`, then `№001 · 4 900 ₽` and a square menu control.
- Mobile: wordmark, release number, menu control.
- Preserve keyboard, focus and route-navigation behavior.

## Home

1. **Campaign hero:** near-black wine background; oversized multi-line `BOOK / FOR / YOU` type; small burgundy `№001`; cropped book-object fragment at an edge; release/price plaque reading `ВЫПУСК №001 — 4 900 ₽` and `СКОРО БУДЕТ ДОСТУПНО`.
2. A narrow wine release statement band.
3. **What is inside:** warm-paper asymmetric grid, physical objects numbered `01–04`, large labels and small renders.
4. Dark, emotional “для другого” callout.
5. Curator text block remains; add an authentic editorial portrait only when one is supplied.

## Edition №001

- A product-story with sticky `001`, oversized title placeholder, vertical/edge price treatment, large cover/object imagery, alternating wine/paper fields and the release plaque.
- Keep neutral title/author placeholders and exact `4 900 ₽`.

## Collection

- Current release is a wide hero panel.
- Other releases are compact editorial rows containing number, title placeholder, status and price.
- Never use a uniform e-commerce card grid.

## Visual system

- Retain tokens `#180F0E`, `#5B2027`, `#F2EADD`, `#FBF7F0`, `#261A17`, `#F4EDE2` and the Cormorant Garamond / Manrope pairing.
- Use borders, alignment and scale rather than decorative gold or ornaments.
- CTA is a typographic plaque, not a conventional brightly colored button.
- 3D assets remain replaceable and secondary.

## Motion and accessibility

- Hero lines reveal in a short stagger; price/release may remain fixed through a short scroll segment; object detail enters through transform/opacity only.
- No global fade-in behavior. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and keep all content visible in that mode.
- Keep 44×44 px targets, focus visibility, semantic headings, route focus/scroll reset, responsive behavior at 375/768/1024/1440, and no horizontal overflow.

## Scope retained

No ordering, cart, payment, forms, analytics, cookies, user-data collection, invented seller details, delivery claims or return claims. Legal placeholders remain unchanged and truthful.
