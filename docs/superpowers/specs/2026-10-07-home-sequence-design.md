# BOOKFORYOU: client home sequence

Purpose: present the physical literary object through the client's numbered mobile storyboard, not a shop feed. This is a bounded revision of the existing home page; routes and data contracts stay intact. User approved the sequence and previously asked not to wait for additional approval and not to use agents.

## Sequence and content

Hero → idea → begin with a question → three questions → how it works → kit → lasting textile envelope → gift → curator → first edition → collection → existing footer.

Use the client's supplied photographs only. Envelope opening and page-turning remain deferred. No backend, purchase flow, email collection or invented portrait. Title/author remain the accepted placeholders until confirmed; the storyboard's book title is not an instruction to change edition data. Explain coordinates as a literary reading route, not divination or guaranteed advice.

## Composition

Preserve oxblood #4b1714, chocolate #160b08, ivory #eee2cc / #f5eddd and brass #b8945b. Preserve Cormorant Garamond display and Manrope body. Short left-aligned headings; readable paragraph measure; no new fonts, dependencies or stock imagery.

Hero footer must occupy its own opaque chocolate strip, outside the image rectangle. Retain the mobile image fade. Kit starts with a large full-set photograph and an explicit four-part inventory, retaining the accessible selector and naturally proportioned detail images. First edition gets a dedicated dark split-screen with a large book photograph, visible price and honest launch status. All other narrative blocks use alternating image-led dark and editorial ivory compositions, not identical feature cards.

## Verification

Pin DOM section order, four process steps, coordinate language, placeholders and functional route links. Check hero image/footer separation at 320/375/768/1024/1575px; no overflow, accessible selector, stable image stage, reduced motion and 200% text. Inspect mobile and desktop screenshots of the revised key sections.
